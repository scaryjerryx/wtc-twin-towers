# NCSTAR Evidence Discoveries

## Date: August 10, 2026

## Most Important Reconstruction Findings

### 1. Both WTC Towers Documented in NCSTAR 1-1

**Finding:** NCSTAR 1-1 contains 191 references to WTC 1 and 181 references to WTC 2.

**Significance:** This partially closes the Tower B structural drawing gap (CG-1). For the first time, the corpus contains detailed documentation of both towers in a single source, not just Tower A.

### 2. Construction Chronology 1966-1969 Is Extensive

**Finding:** NCSTAR 1-1 contains year references:
- 1966: 49 mentions
- 1967: 76 mentions
- 1968: 52 mentions
- 1969: 57 mentions

**Significance:** Sufficient temporal evidence exists to support construction-phase timeline states for the critical steel-erection period.

### 3. Structural Systems Are Comprehensively Documented

**Finding:** NCSTAR 1-1 contains:
- 126 column references
- 84 core references
- 39 spandrel references
- 36 exterior wall references

**Significance:** The structural skeleton of both towers can be modeled with high confidence.

### 4. Floor Plans Exist as Embedded Figures

**Finding:** NCSTAR 1-1 has 3 text references to "floor plan" and the 44MB Appendix C-G is image-based (likely structural drawing reproductions).

**Significance:** Floor plan reproductions are present but require image extraction/OCR, not text extraction.

### 5. Visual Evidence Collection Is Large

**Finding:** NCSTAR 1-8 volumes total ~412MB (Ch1-8 114.6MB + Ch9-AppC 59.4MB + App D-G 127.7MB + App H-M 110.2MB).

**Significance:** This is the largest photographic/visual evidence collection acquired. Likely contains construction photos, exterior photos, and interior photos.

### 6. Egress Plans Show Spatial Layout

**Finding:** NCSTAR 1-7 (4.4MB) focuses on occupant behavior and egress.

**Significance:** Egress plans are floor-plan derivatives showing stairwells, elevator banks, and corridor layouts — useful for spatial modeling.

### 7. Site Plans and Concourse Remain Missing

**Finding:** Zero site plan references and zero concourse references across all NCSTAR reports.

**Significance:** These critical gaps cannot be closed by NIST materials alone.

### 8. Foundation/Slurry Wall Not Documented

**Finding:** Only 2 foundation references in NCSTAR 1-1; zero slurry wall/bathtub references.

**Significance:** The WTC foundation system cannot be modeled from NIST materials.
