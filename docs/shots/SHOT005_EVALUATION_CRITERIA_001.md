# SHOT005 EVALUATION CRITERIA

**Source:** Team D (Critic)

## Review Criteria

1. **Historical Storytelling**: Does the scene accurately depict the mid-60s public engagement with the site via observation decks/windows?
2. **Scale Communication**: Does the street-level viewpoint effectively communicate the dizzying 70-ft drop and the massive scale of the excavation to a layperson?
3. **Public Understanding**: Is it immediately clear that we are looking from a safe, designated public zone into an active, dangerous construction pit?
4. **Viewpoint & Composition**: Does the framing force the viewer to look *through* or *over* the perimeter fence, anchoring them in the pedestrian experience?
5. **Realism**: Basic blockout geometry only (for Blockout pass).

## Evaluation Matrix
The scene will be evaluated on the standard 10-point scale for Technical, Historical, Narrative, and Realism, with a strict cap preventing 10/10 scores unless museum-grade.
