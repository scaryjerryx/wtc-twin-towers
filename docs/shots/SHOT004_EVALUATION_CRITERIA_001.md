# SHOT004 EVALUATION CRITERIA

**Source:** Team D (Critic)

## Review Criteria

1. **Historical Storytelling**: Does the scene accurately capture the essence of Radio Row (electronics/hardware vibe) before its destruction?
2. **Visual Contrast**: Is there a stark, immediately legible contrast between the old, dense urban fabric and the massive, raw new construction pit?
3. **Demolition Narrative**: Can the viewer see the violent transition edge? Are there half-demolished buildings or heavy perimeter hoarding dividing the zones?
4. **Scale Communication**: Does the juxtaposition of small tenement storefronts against heavy industrial machinery emphasize the massive scale of the WTC project?
5. **Realism**: Are the lighting, atmospherics, and textures working together to ground the scene in August 1966? (Blockout pass requires basic geometry fulfillment of these points).

## Evaluation Matrix
The scene will be evaluated on the standard 10-point scale for Technical, Historical, Narrative, and Realism, with a strict cap preventing 10/10 scores unless museum-grade.
