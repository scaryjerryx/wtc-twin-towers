# ROADMAP (002)

The World Trade Center Simulation is divided into 35 canonical "Days" (Milestones), representing the 35-year lifespan of the complex.

## Phase 1: Excavation and Foundation
- **Day 1: August 1966** - Radio Row Demolition & Slurry Wall Excavation (CURRENT PHASE)
- **Day 2: Early 1967** - Bedrock Tie-backs & Bathtub Sealing
- **Day 3: Mid 1967** - Core Column 501 Groundbreaking

## Phase 2: The Steel Rises
- **Day 4: 1968** - The Kangaroo Cranes & Prefabricated Perimeter Trees
- **Day 5: 1969** - Tower A (North Tower) Reaches Floor 50
- **Day 6: 1970** - Topping Out the North Tower
- **Day 7: 1971** - Tower B (South Tower) Topping Out & Aluminum Cladding

## Phase 3: Dedication and Occupation
- **Day 8: April 4, 1973** - Dedication Ceremony on the Austin J. Tobin Plaza
- **Day 9: 1974** - The Philippe Petit High-Wire Walk
- **Day 10: 1975** - Sub-grade Concourse and Mall Opening

## Phase 4: The Golden Era
- **Day 11-15: 1976-1980** - Windows on the World opens; South Tower Outdoor Observation Deck opens.
- **Day 16-25: 1980s** - Evolution of the Plaza; The Sphere; Marriot Hotel (WTC 3) completion.
- **Day 26: 1993** - The 1993 Bombing (Evacuation and Repair)
- **Day 27-34: 1994-2000** - Modernization, Plaza Refurbishment, and peak occupancy.

## Phase 5: The Final Day
- **Day 35: September 2001** - The Final Configuration of the Complex.
