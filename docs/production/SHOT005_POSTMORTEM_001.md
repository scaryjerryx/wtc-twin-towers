# SHOT005 Blockout Postmortem (Public Observation Deck)

## Overview
The SHOT005 Blockout V1 milestone successfully shifted the perspective to the pedestrian street level, illustrating how the public safely engaged with the massive World Trade Center excavation in 1966.

## Successful Public-Viewpoint Composition
- The forced perspective of looking *through* the wire mesh of the observation window perfectly anchors the viewer as a civilian spectator.
- The heavy timber hoarding fence acts as a strong foreground frame, driving the eye toward the chaotic construction pit.

## Successful Scale Communication
- By placing human-scale elements (the fence and window) directly in the foreground, the dizzying 70-foot drop and the massive crawler cranes in the background appear even more imposing and terrifying.

## Reusable Observation System
- The modular hoarding fence with the parameter-driven cut-out window can be placed anywhere along the perimeter (Z = 16 or Z = -16) to instantly generate new street-level vistas.

## Lessons Learned
Restricting the camera movement to the sidewalk and forcing the view through a physical aperture creates a highly immersive, narrative-driven shot without needing to over-detail the entire pit.
