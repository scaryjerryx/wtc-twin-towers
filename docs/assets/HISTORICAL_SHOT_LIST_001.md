# Historical Shot List 001
**Reconstruction Targets: World Trade Center Site 1966-1968**

This document establishes the authoritative visual reconstruction roadmap, shifting the development focus from "building assets" to "recreating documented historical views."

---

## SHOT 001: Church Street Bathtub Overview
*   **Historical Significance:** The primary public vantage point revealing the sheer acreage of the destruction and the depth of the foundation.
*   **Source Photograph(s):** Balthazar Korab collection / HAER wide shots.
*   **Archive Reference(s):** HAER NY-142; PANYNJ Archives.
*   **Date:** [INFERRED] Late 1967.
*   **Confidence Level:** [VERIFIED]
*   **Viewer Objective:** Grasp the 16-acre footprint and the massive depth of the slurry wall perimeter.

### Camera Specification
*   **Camera Position:** Eastern rim (Church Street).
*   **Camera Height:** Eye-level above the hoarding (approx +10 ft relative to rim).
*   **Camera Direction:** West-Northwest toward the Hudson River.
*   **Field of View:** Wide (75-80 degrees).

### Required Visual Elements
*   **What MUST be visible:** The vast empty pit, the entire western slurry wall, bedrock floor.
*   **What MUST NOT be visible:** Tower steel (not yet erected), modern vehicles.

### Scale Cues
*   **Workers/Vehicles:** Tiny crawler cranes on the pit floor.
*   **Buildings:** The rim of surviving Lower Manhattan framing the edge.

### Success Criterion
*   A viewer should conclude: *"This is a massive, city-scale crater, not just a building foundation."*

## SHOT 003: Icanda Slurry Wall Operation
*   **Historical Significance:** The pioneering Italian trenching technology that made the bathtub possible in wet landfill.
*   **Source Photograph(s):** *Engineering News-Record* coverage.
*   **Archive Reference(s):** PANYNJ Archives (1966-1967).
*   **Date:** [VERIFIED] Winter 1966/1967.
*   **Confidence Level:** [VERIFIED]
*   **Viewer Objective:** Recognize specialized diaphragm-wall engineering vs. generic dirt excavation.

### Camera Specification
*   **Camera Position:** Ground level near the West Street perimeter.
*   **Camera Height:** Eye-level.
*   **Camera Direction:** Looking East toward Radio Row.
*   **Field of View:** Standard (65 degrees).

### Required Visual Elements
*   **What MUST be visible:** Vertical guide-mast, clamshell grab, 3-foot slurry trench, bentonite tanks/hoses.
*   **What MUST NOT be visible:** Lattice crane booms being used for digging.

### Scale Cues
*   **Workers/Vehicles:** Operators next to the mud tanks.
*   **Buildings:** Radio Row storefronts in the immediate background.

### Success Criterion
*   A viewer should conclude: *"This specialized machine is injecting liquid into a precise vertical trench."*

## SHOT 005: Tie-Back Grid Wall
*   **Historical Significance:** The structural mechanism (replacing internal cross-bracing) that held back the Hudson River.
*   **Source Photograph(s):** *Engineering News-Record*.
*   **Archive Reference(s):** NAE Memorial Tributes (Tamaro).
*   **Date:** [INFERRED] 1968.
*   **Confidence Level:** [VERIFIED]
*   **Viewer Objective:** Comprehend the brutalist, repetitive engineering required to secure the perimeter.

### Camera Specification
*   **Camera Position:** Elevated within the pit or using a telephoto lens from the rim.
*   **Camera Height:** Mid-wall (Y=-35).
*   **Camera Direction:** Grazing angle along the slurry wall.
*   **Field of View:** Narrow/Telephoto (35 degrees) to compress the grid.

### Required Visual Elements
*   **What MUST be visible:** Thousands of protruding angled steel tie-back caps, vertical panel seams.
*   **What MUST NOT be visible:** Flat grey textures without physical cap geometry.

### Scale Cues
*   **Lighting:** Harsh directional sunlight casting repetitive diagonal shadows from every cap.

### Success Criterion
*   A viewer should conclude: *"That concrete wall is pinned back by thousands of massive engineered anchors."*

## SHOT 010: Slurry Wall Close Detail
*   **Historical Significance:** Documents the physical, tactile reality of the pioneering "Milan method" concrete pours.
*   **Source Photograph(s):** 9/11 Memorial Museum exhibits.
*   **Archive Reference(s):** PANYNJ Archives.
*   **Date:** [INFERRED] Post-1968.
*   **Confidence Level:** [VERIFIED]
*   **Viewer Objective:** See the imperfections, the dirt, and the water seepage that proves the wall's function.

### Camera Specification
*   **Camera Position:** Inches away from the slurry wall face.
*   **Camera Height:** Eye-level.
*   **Camera Direction:** Straight at the concrete.
*   **Field of View:** Narrow (40 degrees).

### Required Visual Elements
*   **What MUST be visible:** Rough concrete texture, vertical panel seam gap, dark moisture stains, rust streaking.
*   **What MUST NOT be visible:** Smooth, clean, perfectly flat grey textures.

### Scale Cues
*   **Lighting:** Harsh micro-shadowing emphasizing the bumpy, rough cast surface.

### Success Criterion
*   A viewer should conclude: *"This concrete was poured blindly against raw earth and is holding back muddy water."*
