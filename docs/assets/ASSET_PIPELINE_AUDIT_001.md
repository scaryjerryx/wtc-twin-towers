# ASSET PIPELINE AUDIT 001

## 1. Historical construction equipment
* **Historical Importance:** Critical. The World Trade Center used unique Australian Favco "kangaroo" cranes, which were essential to its construction methodology.
* **Visual Impact:** Very High. These cranes were iconic silhouettes on the skyline during the late 60s and early 70s.
* **Source Options:** Turbosquid, CGTrader, custom modeling.
* **Acquisition Method:** Build ourselves / Purchase base models.
* **License Considerations:** Commercial license if purchasing; avoid trademarked logos unless cleared.
* **Implementation Difficulty:** High (requires rigging for animation).
* **Estimated Realism Gain:** 9/10
* **Decision:** **Build ourselves** (or modify purchased base models). The Favco cranes are too specific to find accurate pre-made models.

## 2. Steel materials
* **Historical Importance:** Critical. The exterior columns and floor trusses were the defining architectural and structural features.
* **Visual Impact:** Very High. The aluminum-clad steel columns are the most visible part of the finished towers.
* **Source Options:** Megascans, Substance Source, custom procedural materials.
* **Acquisition Method:** Generate (Procedural).
* **License Considerations:** Standard material licenses.
* **Implementation Difficulty:** Medium.
* **Estimated Realism Gain:** 9/10
* **Decision:** **Generate** using Substance Designer to ensure exact rust, primer (red oxide), and aluminum finishes.

## 3. Concrete materials
* **Historical Importance:** High. Used extensively in the foundation, the "bathtub" slurry wall, and floor slabs.
* **Visual Impact:** High for excavation and foundation phases.
* **Source Options:** Quixel Megascans.
* **Acquisition Method:** Download.
* **License Considerations:** Quixel license terms.
* **Implementation Difficulty:** Low.
* **Estimated Realism Gain:** 8/10
* **Decision:** **Download**. High-quality photorealistic concrete is readily available.

## 4. Mud and excavation materials
* **Historical Importance:** High. The 1.2 million cubic yards of excavated dirt were used to create Battery Park City.
* **Visual Impact:** High for the early phases of the site.
* **Source Options:** Quixel Megascans.
* **Acquisition Method:** Download / Generate blends.
* **License Considerations:** Quixel license terms.
* **Implementation Difficulty:** Medium (requires good blending and displacement).
* **Estimated Realism Gain:** 7/10
* **Decision:** **Download** and blend in-engine.

## 5. Radio Row storefront assets
* **Historical Importance:** High. Radio Row was demolished to make way for the WTC; its memory is vital for the historical context.
* **Visual Impact:** High for street-level realism pre-1966.
* **Source Options:** KitBash3D, custom modeling, photo scanning of vintage electronics.
* **Acquisition Method:** Purchase / Build ourselves.
* **License Considerations:** Need to create fictionalized or cleared vintage branding.
* **Implementation Difficulty:** High (requires many unique props).
* **Estimated Realism Gain:** 8/10
* **Decision:** **Purchase** base modular storefronts, **Build ourselves** for specific signage and hero props.

## 6. Worker assets
* **Historical Importance:** Medium. Represents the human scale and era-specific safety standards.

* **Implementation Difficulty:** High (if animated).
