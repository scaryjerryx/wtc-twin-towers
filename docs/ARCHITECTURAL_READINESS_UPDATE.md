# Architectural Readiness Update

## Date: August 11, 2026

## Executive Summary

The acquisition of 211 architectural blueprints from 911Research.net fundamentally transforms the WTC Reconstruction Project. This corpus provides the first comprehensive architectural documentation of Tower A, closing the most critical evidence gap (CG-2: Architectural Floor Plans) and enabling detailed floor-by-floor reconstruction.

**Key Achievement:** Tower A is now architecturally documented from Sub-Level 5 through the 110th Floor roof, with special spaces including Windows on the World, the Observation Deck, and Sky Lobby 44 fully captured.

## 4. What Can Be Reconstructed Today

### 4.1 Using ONLY This Corpus

The following elements can be reconstructed using only the 911Research blueprint corpus:

#### ✅ FULLY SUPPORTED (High Confidence)

1. **Complete Floor-by-Floor Architectural Layout**
   - Every floor from Sub-Level 5 to 110th Floor
   - Room layouts, corridor positions, wall locations
   - Door placements and window positions
   - **Evidence:** 89 floor plans

2. **Windows on the World Restaurant (107th Floor)**
   - Complete restaurant layout
   - Kitchen locations, dining areas, bar positions
   - Integration with observation deck
   - **Evidence:** A-A-165, A-A-166, A-A-167, A-A-168

3. **Observation Deck (107th Floor)**
   - Full observation deck layout
   - Viewing areas, access points
   - **Evidence:** A-A-167, A-A-168

4. **Sky Lobby 44 (44th Floor)**
   - Complete lobby layout
   - Escalator positions, waiting areas
   - Zone transfer points
   - **Evidence:** A-A-72, A-A-73

5. **All Mechanical Floors**
   - 7th-8th, 41st-42nd, 75th-76th, 108th-109th
   - Equipment layouts, shaft positions
   - **Evidence:** 10 mechanical floor plans

6. **Elevator and Escalator Systems**
   - All three zones (1-3)
   - Pit plans, riser plans, machine rooms, elevations
   - Freight and shuttle elevators
   - **Evidence:** 26 LA drawings

7. **Foundation and Sub-Level Structure**
   - Sub-Levels 1-5 fully documented
   - Service Level documented
   - **Evidence:** 9 sub-level drawings

8. **Tower Vertical Sections**
   - Four sections (A, B, C, D)
   - Full-height structural organization
   - **Evidence:** A-A-177, 178, 179, 180

9. **TV Mast Support System**
   - Complete support structure
   - **Evidence:** SA401, 402, 403, 404

1. **Typical Office Floors (9th-106th)**
   - Typical plans cover multiple floors
   - Some individual floor plans available
   - **Evidence:** Multiple A-A drawings

2. **Stair Systems**
   - Four stair detail drawings
   - Not all locations fully detailed
   - **Evidence:** A-A-67, 68, 125, 126

1. **Structural Frame**

2. **Exterior Curtain Wall**
   - Requires façade drawings

3. **Sky Lobby 78**
   - Not in this collection
   - Upper zone access unknown

4. **Construction Sequence**
