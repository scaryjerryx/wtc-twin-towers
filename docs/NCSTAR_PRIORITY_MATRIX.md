# NCSTAR Priority Matrix

## Date

August 10, 2026

## Purpose

Identify the smallest subset of NIST NCSTAR reports that would move reconstruction readiness the most, by mapping each report to the evidence categories it covers.

---

## NCSTAR Report Series Overview

The NIST Federal Building and Fire Safety Investigation of the World Trade Center Disaster produced the following report series:

| Report | Title | Volumes |
|---|---|---|
| NCSTAR 1-1 | Design, Construction, and Maintenance of Structural and Life Safety Systems | 1 (with appendices A-L) |
| NCSTAR 1-2 | Baseline Structural Performance and Aircraft Impact Damage Analysis | 1 |
| NCSTAR 1-3 | Mechanical and Metallurgical Analysis of Structural Steel | 1 |
| NCSTAR 1-4 | Initial Passive Fire Protection | 1 |
| NCSTAR 1-5 | Reconstruction of the Fires in the World Trade Center Towers | 1 |
| NCSTAR 1-6 | Structural Fire Response and Probable Collapse Sequence | 1 |
| NCSTAR 1-7 | Occupant Behavior, Egress, and Emergency Communications | 1 |
| NCSTAR 1-8 | Visual Evidence, Damage Estimates, and Timeline Analysis | 1 |
| NCSTAR 1-9 | Final Report on the Collapse of the World Trade Center Towers | 1 |
| NCSTAR 1-9A | Global Structural Analysis of WTC 7 Response to Fires and Debris Impact | 1 (already acquired) |
| NCSTAR 1-A | Final Report on the Collapse of World Trade Center Building 7 | 1 |

## Priority Matrix

### Rank 1 — CRITICAL (Acquire First)

#### NCSTAR 1-1: Design, Construction, and Maintenance of Structural and Life Safety Systems

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Report Number** | NCSTAR 1-1 (with Appendices A through L) |
| **Topic** | Complete design and construction documentation of the WTC structural systems |
| **Reconstruction Value** | **Critical** — This is the single most important report for reconstruction. It contains the design and construction history, structural system descriptions, and as-built documentation. |
| **Evidence Categories Covered** | Floor plans, site plans, tower architectural layouts, exterior wall schedules, foundation layouts, construction chronology, structural drawings, column layouts, beam schedules, connection details, mechanical floor layouts, elevator shafts, stairwells |
| **Gaps Addressed** | CG-2 (Floor Plans), CG-3 (Site Plans), CG-4 (Exterior Wall Schedules), IG-6 (Foundation) |
| **Key Content** | Appendix A: WTC 1 and WTC 2 design description; Appendix B: Structural design criteria; Appendix C: Construction history; Appendix D: Maintenance history; Appendix E-L: Detailed structural drawings and floor plans |
| **Estimated Size** | 50-200MB (with appendices) |

#### NCSTAR 1-2: Baseline Structural Performance and Aircraft Impact Damage Analysis

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Report Number** | NCSTAR 1-2 |
| **Topic** | Baseline structural performance of the WTC towers before aircraft impact |
| **Reconstruction Value** | **Critical** — Contains the "as-designed" structural model of the towers, including floor framing plans, column schedules, and connection details that define the structural geometry needed for 3D reconstruction. |
| **Evidence Categories Covered** | Tower structural layouts, column schedules, beam schedules, floor framing plans, connection details, structural models |
| **Gaps Addressed** | CG-1 (Tower B structural — partial, as NCSTAR 1-2 covers both towers), CG-4 (Exterior Wall — structural analysis) |
| **Estimated Size** | 50-100MB |

#### NCSTAR 1-8: Visual Evidence, Damage Estimates, and Timeline Analysis

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Report Number** | NCSTAR 1-8 |
| **Topic** | Visual evidence collection, damage documentation, and timeline analysis of the WTC disaster |
| **Reconstruction Value** | **Critical** — Contains the most comprehensive collection of visual evidence (photographs and video stills) of the WTC complex, including pre-impact exterior views, interior views, and construction-era photographs used as reference. |
| **Evidence Categories Covered** | Construction chronology, visual evidence, exterior photographs, interior photographs, timeline of events, damage documentation |
| **Gaps Addressed** | IG-1 (Construction Photographs — partial), IG-2 (Interior Photographs — partial), IG-5 (Architectural Elevations — visual reference) |
| **Estimated Size** | 50-100MB |

### Rank 2 — IMPORTANT (Acquire Second)

#### NCSTAR 1-3: Mechanical and Metallurgical Analysis of Structural Steel

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Report Number** | NCSTAR 1-3 |
| **Topic** | Analysis of structural steel recovered from the WTC, including mechanical properties and metallurgical analysis |
| **Reconstruction Value** | **Important** — Provides material specifications (steel grades, yield strengths) for structural elements. Useful for material-accurate reconstruction but not for spatial geometry. |
| **Evidence Categories Covered** | Material specifications, steel grades, mechanical properties, metallurgical analysis |
| **Gaps Addressed** | None directly, but supports structural accuracy |
| **Estimated Size** | 30-50MB |

#### NCSTAR 1-6: Structural Fire Response and Probable Collapse Sequence

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Report Number** | NCSTAR 1-6 |
| **Topic** | Analysis of the structural fire response and probable collapse sequence of the WTC towers |
| **Reconstruction Value** | **Important** — Contains detailed structural models of the towers used for fire analysis. These models include floor layouts, column grids, and connection details that could be used for reconstruction geometry. |
| **Evidence Categories Covered** | Structural models, floor layouts, connection details, fire protection systems, SFRM thickness data |
| **Gaps Addressed** | IG-4 (MEP — partial, fire protection systems) |
| **Estimated Size** | 50-100MB |

#### NCSTAR 1-7: Occupant Behavior, Egress, and Emergency Communications

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Report Number** | NCSTAR 1-7 |
| **Topic** | Analysis of occupant behavior, egress, and emergency communications during the WTC disaster |
| **Reconstruction Value** | **Important** — Contains egress plans showing stairwell locations, elevator banks, and corridor layouts. These are effectively floor plan derivatives that show the architectural spatial organization. |
| **Evidence Categories Covered** | Egress plans, stairwell layouts, elevator bank locations, corridor configurations, tenant space boundaries |
| **Gaps Addressed** | CG-2 (Floor Plans — partial, egress plans show spatial layout) |
| **Estimated Size** | 50-100MB |

| Field | Value |
