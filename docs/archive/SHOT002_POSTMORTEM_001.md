# SHOT002 Postmortem (Suspended PATH Tubes)

## Overview
SHOT002 focused on capturing the immense scale and structural challenge of suspending the active PATH transit tubes over the 70-ft bathtub excavation.

## Final Archived Scores (Calibrated)
- **Technical**: 8/10
- **Historical**: 7/10
- **Narrative**: 9/10
- **Realism**: 7/10

## What Worked
- **Structural Blockout**: The dual tubes, cradles, and X-bracing successfully communicated the engineering reality of the underpinning.
- **Atmospherics**: Deep haze amplified the perceived depth of the pit.
- **Metal Textures**: Integrating the Metal035 PBR material provided realistic rusting and wear to the cast-iron and steel elements.

## What Failed
- **No major failures**: The pipeline established in SHOT009 accelerated asset integration, though further historical refinement on the exact suspension cribbing could yield higher scores under the new strict calibration.

## Reusable Systems Created
- **PATH Tube Assembly**: A scalable, parametric component for the transit lines that can be extended or modified for other shots.
