## Executive Summary

Following the governance audit and Tower B readiness validation (adjusting Tower B direct-evidence readiness to **25%** and complex readiness to **~60–62%**), this document establishes the strategic evidence acquisition plan for closing the 4 critical reconstruction blockers:

- **CG-1:** Tower B (South Tower) Structural Drawings
- **CG-2B:** Tower B Architectural Floor Plans
- **CG-3:** Site Plans & Austin J. Tobin Plaza Documentation
- **CG-4:** Tower A Exterior Wall Schedules (Floors 10–110)

By executing these targeted campaigns, overall World Trade Center complex readiness will advance from **~60% to ~85%+**, unlocking Prototype 0.1 multi-tower 3D reconstruction.

## 1. Detailed Gap Analysis

### Gap CG-1: Tower B (South Tower) Structural Drawings

1. **Missing Evidence:** High-resolution structural engineering sheet scans (equivalent to the 895 PNGs in AA20a1 for Tower A) showing floor framing plans, column schedules, spandrel beam connections, core framing, and truss geometry for WTC 2.
2. **Reconstruction Importance:** Essential for modeling verified 3D structural steel geometry for the South Tower. Under **Principle 7 (*No Symmetry Assumptions*)**, Tower B cannot inherit Tower A framing without direct evidence.
3. **Target Repositories & Archives:**
   - **Internet Archive:** Uploader collections associated with `AA20a1` (e.g., `AA20b1` or sister uploads).
   - **NIST FOIA Public Repositories:** Structural drawing releases from the NCSTAR 1-1 / 1-2 investigation.
   - **Leslie E. Robertson Associates (LERA) Archives:** Structural engineer of record files.
   - **Port Authority of NY/NJ Engineering Archives:** Contract drawing releases.
4. **Target Search Terms:**  
   `"WTC 2 structural drawings"`, `"South Tower structural sheets"`, `"AA20b1"`, `"AA20"`, `"WTC Tower B framing plans"`, `"LERA South Tower structural"`, `"WTC 2 column schedules"`.
5. **Existing Corpus Evidence:**  
   NCSTAR 1-1 (~181 text references to WTC 2 structural system), NCSTAR 1-2 baseline summary framing diagrams, NCSTAR 1-8 visual evidence photos (657 images). Zero dedicated structural sheet scans.
6. **Expected Readiness Gain:** Tower B readiness increases **25% → 60%** (+35%). Complex readiness increases **+5%**.
7. **Likelihood of Acquisition:** **High** (High probability that sister drawing collections exist in public Internet Archive or NIST FOIA uploads).

---

### Gap CG-2B: Tower B Architectural Floor Plans

1. **Missing Evidence:** Complete set of architectural floor plans, core plans, door schedules, finish schedules, and section drawings for Tower B floors 1–110 and sub-levels.
2. **Reconstruction Importance:** Required to model interior space layouts, office zones, corridor networks, elevator bank hoistways, stairwells, Sky Lobby 78, and the outdoor rooftop observation deck.
3. **Target Repositories & Archives:**
   - **NYC Department of Buildings (DOB):** Building permit filings and microfilmed architectural plans.
   - **Port Authority of NY/NJ Archives:** Architectural drawing collections.
   - **Minoru Yamasaki Associates Archives:** University architectural library collections (e.g., Wayne State University, Avery Architectural Library).
   - **Emery Roth & Sons Archives:** Architect of record working drawings.
4. **Target Search Terms:**  
   `"WTC 2 floor plans"`, `"South Tower architectural drawings"`, `"WTC 2 core layout"`, `"Yamasaki South Tower plans"`, `"WTC Sky Lobby 78 architectural"`, `"Emery Roth WTC 2"`.
5. **Existing Corpus Evidence:** **0 architectural floor plans in corpus** for Tower B. (Tower A has 211 blueprints).
6. **Expected Readiness Gain:** Tower B readiness increases **60% → 85%** (+25%). Complex readiness increases **+5%**.
7. **Likelihood of Acquisition:** **Medium** (Requires formal FOIA/records requests or archive browsing across architectural repositories).

---

### Gap CG-3: Site Plans & Austin J. Tobin Plaza Documentation

1. **Missing Evidence:** Civil site plans, paving and landscape drawings, Tobin Plaza fountain structural details, concourse mall interface plans, street-level entrance profiles, and site utility layout drawings.
2. **Reconstruction Importance:** The 16-acre superblock and Austin J. Tobin Plaza connect all 7 buildings, concourse levels, and transit lines. Required to elevate Site readiness (40%) and Plaza readiness (25%) to >75%.
3. **Target Repositories & Archives:**
   - **NYC Department of City Planning & DOB Archives:** Master site plan filings.
   - **Port Authority of NY/NJ Archives:** Plaza construction and concourse engineering drawings.
   - **Library of Congress (HABS/HAER Collections):** Historic American Buildings Survey drawings and photographs.
   - **Minoru Yamasaki Associates Site Archives:** Plaza design renderings and civil plans.
4. **Target Search Terms:**  
   `"WTC site plan"`, `"Austin J. Tobin Plaza drawings"`, `"World Trade Center plaza level plan"`, `"WTC concourse layout"`, `"Yamasaki plaza details"`, `"WTC superblock civil drawings"`.
5. **Existing Corpus Evidence:** 6 site plan SVG/PNG files (basic footprints and site boundary outlines), Sub-level 1–5 foundation core plans (`A-A-8` to `A-A-16`).
6. **Expected Readiness Gain:** Site readiness increases **40% → 80%** (+40%); Plaza readiness increases **25% → 75%** (+50%). Complex readiness increases **+7%**.
7. **Likelihood of Acquisition:** **High** (Plaza and site plans were widely published in Port Authority reports, architectural journals, and HABS/HAER surveys).

---

### Gap CG-4: Tower A Exterior Wall Schedules (Floors 10–110)

1. **Missing Evidence:** Exterior wall panel schedules, spandrel thickness specifications, aluminum column cover dimension sheets, and window module transition drawings for Tower A floors 10 through 110.
2. **Reconstruction Importance:** Exterior wall panel system changed at mechanical floors (7–8, 41–42, 75–76, 108–109) and varied by height. Required to model the full facade envelope with 95%+ confidence.
3. **Target Repositories & Archives:**
   - **NCSTAR 1-1 Appendices:** Detailed structural manufacturer tables and curtain wall appendices.
   - **LERA / Skilling Engineering Archives:** Exterior wall panel fabrication schedules.
   - **Cupples Products Corp Archives:** Aluminum curtain wall fabricator records.
   - **Port Authority Construction Records:** As-built wall panel schedules.
4. **Target Search Terms:**  
   `"WTC exterior wall schedule"`, `"Tower A wall panels floors 10-110"`, `"WTC spandrel schedules"`, `"Cupples WTC exterior curtain wall"`, `"WTC column cover drawings"`.
5. **Existing Corpus Evidence:** Exterior wall XLS schedules covering floors 1–9 only (`WTC_CORPUS/exterior-wall/`), general architectural elevation sheets `A-A-1` through `A-A-7`.
6. **Expected Readiness Gain:** Tower A building envelope readiness increases **70% → 95%** (+25%). Complex readiness increases **+2%**.
7. **Likelihood of Acquisition:** **High** (NCSTAR 1-1 appendices contain extensive curtain wall and exterior column schedule tables).

## 3. Recommended Search Campaigns

### Campaign 1: Internet Archive & FOIA Automated Sweeps (CG-1 & CG-3)
- **Target Subsystem:** `agents/discovery/`
- **Execution Steps:**
  1. Add source templates for Internet Archive API, NIST FOIA repositories, and Library of Congress HABS/HAER collections in `agents/discovery/sources.json`.
  2. Run query generator:  
     `python -m agents.discovery.main --build-searches`
  3. Execute automated candidate finder targeting keywords `"AA20"`, `"WTC 2 structural"`, `"Tobin Plaza site plan"`.
  4. Manual review and promotion:  
     `python -m agents.discovery.manual_promote`

### Campaign 2: NCSTAR PDF Deep Image Extraction (CG-4)
- **Target Subsystem:** `agents/processors/`
- **Execution Steps:**
  1. Run `pdftoppm` high-resolution image extraction on `NCSTAR_1-1_Appendix_C-G.pdf`.
  2. Route extracted sheet images to `agents/metadata/ai_analyze.py` to identify exterior wall schedule tables for floors 10–110.
  3. Convert extracted tables into structured XLS schedules in `WTC_CORPUS/exterior-wall/`.

### Campaign 3: Formal Records Request Campaign (CG-2B)
- **Target Subsystem:** Manual Records Officer
- **Execution Steps:**
  1. Prepare formal FOIL request to NYC Department of Buildings for WTC 2 (South Tower) microfilmed architectural permit drawings.
  2. Submit records inquiry to Port Authority of NY/NJ Archives for Emery Roth & Sons WTC 2 architectural drawing sets.
