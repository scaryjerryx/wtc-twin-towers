## Answers to Assessment Questions

### A. Is this collection redundant?

**Partially.** Two of the 23 PDFs (WTCI-000721-L and WTCI-000037-L) are already in the corpus. The unidentified Books 2-6 and 20 may overlap with existing WTCI books. However, four WTCI identifiers (000030-L, 000031-L, 000059-L, 000262-L) are **not** in the existing corpus and represent new evidence.

**Verdict: ~30-40% redundant, ~60-70% new or supplementary.**

### B. Does it increase reconstruction readiness?

**Yes, modestly.** The collection adds 4 new WTCI drawing books (1,759+ TIFFs + 2 PDFs) not currently held. This supplements the existing structural drawing collection and may partially address OG-1 (missing drawing books). If WTCI-000262-L contains Tower B sheets, the gain could be +3-5%.

**Verdict: +2-5% readiness gain, depending on Tower B content.**

### C. Does it contain missing Tower B evidence?

**Unknown — requires content inspection.** WTCI-000262-L (1,364 TIFFs) is the most promising candidate. Some WTCI drawing books cover both towers. However, the existing WTCI collection is heavily Tower A-focused, and there is no guarantee this collection differs.

**Verdict: Possible but unconfirmed. WTCI-000262-L is the best candidate for Tower B content.**

### D. Does it contain floor-plan or architectural information not currently represented?

**Unlikely.** WTCI drawing books are structural engineering documents — they contain framing plans, column schedules, beam schedules, and connection details. They do not contain architectural floor plans (walls, rooms, corridors, tenant spaces). This collection supplements CG-1 (structural drawings) but does not address CG-2 (architectural floor plans).

**Verdict: No architectural floor plans. Structural framing plans only.**

### E. Should this collection become part of the core reconstruction corpus?

**Yes, with prioritization.** The collection should be:
1. **Extracted** — unzip to `WTC_CORPUS/structural-drawings/skyscraperforum/`
2. **Deduplicated** — remove WTCI-000721-L and WTCI-000037-L (already in corpus)
3. **Prioritized for inspection** — WTCI-000262-L (Book 7) first, due to size (1,364 TIFFs) and potential Tower B content
4. **Cataloged** — identify the WTCI identifiers for Books 2-6 and 20
5. **Compared** — cross-reference against existing WTCI books to identify true duplicates

**Verdict: Yes — include in corpus after deduplication. Prioritize WTCI-000262-L for Tower B content inspection.**
