## Content Assessment

### What NCSTAR 1-8 Contains

NCSTAR 1-8 is the NIST "Visual Evidence, Damage Estimates, and Timeline Analysis" report. It documents:
- Pre-impact conditions of the WTC complex
- Aircraft impact damage
- Fire spread and progression
- Structural damage progression
- Collapse sequence
- Debris field documentation
- Timeline of events on September 11, 2001

### Limitations for Reconstruction

1. **Post-collapse focus:** NCSTAR 1-8 documents the disaster and its aftermath, not the original construction. Most photographs show damage, fire, and collapse — not the intact building.

2. **No construction chronology:** The report covers September 11, 2001, not 1966-1973 construction.

3. **Damage-centric:** Images are selected to document damage progression, not architectural features.

4. **Low resolution JPGs:** The 97 JPG files are small (3-26KB) — likely thumbnails or low-resolution figures, not high-quality photographs.

5. **PPM format:** The 560 PPM files are full-page scans at 6.1MB each — these are the actual content but require conversion to view.
