## Executive Summary

**CRITICAL DISCOVERY:** The 911Research.net website hosts **261 high-resolution architectural blueprints** of the World Trade Center North Tower (WTC 1), released by a whistleblower in March 2007. These drawings include:

- **Complete floor plans** from Sub-Level 5 through 110th Floor (roof)
- **Core plans** for every floor showing elevator banks, stairs, and mechanical systems
- **Mechanical floor plans** (floors 7-8, 41-42, 75-76, 108-109)
- **Observation Deck plans** (107th floor - Windows on the World restaurant level)
- **Skylobby plans** (44th and 78th floors)
- **TV Mast support details** and structural drawings
- **Tower sections** (Sections A, B, C, D)
- **Elevator and escalator details** for all zones
- **Foundation and basement plans**

**Reconstruction Value:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **CRITICAL** - These blueprints provide the exact architectural layout needed for floor-level reconstruction modeling.
