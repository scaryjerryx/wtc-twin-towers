## Answer: "What Reconstruction Elements Could Be Modeled Today?"

### ✅ Modelable Today

1. **WTC 1 (North Tower) Structural Skeleton**
   - Column grid and layout (126 column references in NCSTAR 1-1)
   - Spandrel beam system (39 references)
   - Core structural system (84 references)
   - Exterior wall system for floors 1-9 (XLS spreadsheet)
   - Floor framing plans (AA20a1 structural sheets, 895 PNGs)
   - Construction chronology 1966-1969 (49-76 year references)

2. **WTC 2 (South Tower) Structural Skeleton (Partial)**
   - Structural system references (181 WTC 2 mentions in NCSTAR 1-1)
   - Baseline structural model (NCSTAR 1-2)
   - No dedicated structural sheet collection (unlike AA20a1 for WTC 1)

3. **WTC 7 Structural System (Partial)**
   - Column analysis (528 column references in NCSTAR 1-9A)
   - Core system (117 core references)
   - Construction specifications (WTC7 OEM spec manual)

4. **Construction Timeline (1966-1969)**
   - Site preparation and foundation (49 references to 1966)
   - Steel erection (76 references to 1967)
   - Tower A steel (52 references to 1968)
   - Tower A topping out, Tower B steel (57 references to 1969)

### ⚠️ Partially Modelable

5. **Exterior Wall System (WTC 1, floors 1-9 only)**
   - Panel types and dimensions from XLS spreadsheet
   - Exterior wall references in NCSTAR 1-1 (36 mentions)
   - Upper floors not documented

6. **Building Footprints and Relationships**
   - Inferable from NCSTAR 1-1 structural references
   - Wikimedia site plan SVGs (low-resolution only)

### ❌ Not Modelable Today

7. Architectural floor plans (any building)
8. Interior spaces (any building)
9. Plaza layout
10. Concourse layout
11. Foundation/slurry wall
12. MEP systems
13. Buildings 3-6
14. Observation Deck interior
15. Windows on the World interior
16. Post-1970 construction chronology
17. 1993 bombing damage and repairs
18. Tenant layouts and operational history

### Summary

**The project can model a structural skeleton of WTC 1 (North Tower) today**, using the AA20a1 structural sheets, exterior wall schedules, and NCSTAR 1-1 structural documentation. A partial structural skeleton of WTC 2 is also possible using NCSTAR 1-1 and 1-2 references. Construction chronology for 1966-1969 is supported by extensive year references in NCSTAR 1-1.

**The project cannot model architectural spaces, interiors, the plaza, the concourse, or any building beyond the structural skeleton level.** Architectural floor plans remain the single most critical missing evidence category.
